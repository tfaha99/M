About Us

Who We Are

Rahma Medicines is a non-profit charity dedicated to providing essential medicines and medical support to families who cannot afford the cost of treatment. We believe access to medicine is a basic right, not a privilege.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce the suffering caused by lack of access to medicine. We work with donors, pharmacies and volunteers to deliver medicines and treatment support directly to patients in need.

Our Values

  • Compassion — we treat every person with dignity and care.
  • Transparency — every donation is tracked and used responsibly.
  • Community — we work hand in hand with local volunteers and partners.
